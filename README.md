**A public list of North American LinkedIn Open Networkers (LIONs).**

Open networkers want to maximize their own connections so they typically accept connection requests from anyone. You click through, open a profile, and send the request yourself.

This is a curated list, not an app or a bot. Not affiliated with LinkedIn.

This list is free. Brought to you by [EasyIn](https://easyin.io), a LinkedIn CRM built for sales, recruiting, and networking.

---

## Why they're more likely to accept

These aren't random profiles. Everyone here identifies as a **LinkedIn Open Networker (LION)** — someone who has already said they welcome connection requests from people they don't know.

That's the whole point of the list: you aren't spending your scarce connection requests on strangers who ignore or mark them as spam.

It's not a guarantee they'll accept. You still send a normal request yourself.

---

## Who it's for

People who want more LinkedIn connections in the US and Canada, without paying for some sketchy service.

## How to use it

1. Pick someone from the list.
2. Open their LinkedIn profile.
3. Send a connection request.
4. Repeat until you're satisfied with your connections.

If a profile won't open or you can't send a request yet, skip it. After a few people accept, the rest of the list is usually easier to reach.
